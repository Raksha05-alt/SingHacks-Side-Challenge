# submissions/

Every candidate's work for this use case lives here, one folder per
person:

```
usecase-2-production-incident-investigator/
└── submissions/
    └── <your-name>/
        ├── solution.py
        ├── answers.json
        └── README.md
```

- **`solution.py`** — your `investigate()` implementation (copy
  `../starter/solution.py` in as your starting point).
- **`answers.json`** — your `investigate()`'s output for both incidents.
  See `../README.md`, "What you submit", for the exact shape.
- **`README.md`** — your design write-up, understanding of the problem,
  approach/tradeoffs, and your name, phone number, and email. See the
  root [README.md](../../README.md)'s "Submission requirements" for what
  this needs to contain.

Create your own `<your-name>/` folder here and add all three files —
don't edit anything else in this repo. Evaluation is manual: the
organizer reads your `answers.json` against a private answer key, and
your `solution.py` + `README.md` alongside it.
